# Timezone Information

<!-- llms-safe:start -->

## What CreepJS reports

Reads the resolved timezone and locale, UTC offset, and available Intl date, number, collation, and language formatting behavior.

## How to interpret it

Change one operating-system timezone or browser language setting, then compare the relevant fields. A timezone differs from an IP-derived geographic location.

## Limits

Timezone does not reveal a precise location. Seasonal offsets, locale data, and browser support can change parts of the response independently. A single signal does not identify a person.

## Test the signal

Keep the live result for comparison, change one relevant setting, then run again in the same tab. Compare the fields before interpreting a changed signature.
